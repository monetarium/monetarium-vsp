VSP Overview

A Voting Service Provider is a non-custodial service that lets you stake without needing to keep your wallet online. It maintains a pool of always-online voting wallets which vote your Varta (VAR) tickets on your behalf, in exchange for a small fee. The service never holds, manages, or has access to any user funds. Visit monetarium.world to find out more about Monetarium, or browse tickets and votes in the block explorer.
